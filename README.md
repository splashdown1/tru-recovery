### 2. Eye Optics Core (saved mathematical models)

Stored for continuity:

**Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)** for blink / openness:

```
EAR = (‖p2 − p6‖ + ‖p3 − p5‖) / (2 · ‖p1 − p4‖)
```

Where p1…p6 are the six periocular landmarks. EAR drops toward 0 on blink.

**3D Gaze Geometry**
- Eye modeled as sphere ≈ 24 mm diameter.
- Pupil appears as ellipse when off-axis.
- Optical axis from eye rotation center through 3D pupil center.
- Visual axis offset by kappa angle (typically ~5° horizontal).

**Pupil size measurement** is geometrically biased by gaze angle; foreshortening corrections are required for accurate pupillometry.

**Basic optical constants**
- Cornea radius ≈ 7.8 mm
- Entrance pupil is the virtual image of the iris through the cornea.

These models are geometry and optics only. They do not claim mind-reading or guaranteed intent detection.
